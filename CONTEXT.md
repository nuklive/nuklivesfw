# Course Platform

A platform where learners buy courses, watch lessons, and get their money back within a limited window.

## Language

**Purchase**:
A learner's payment for access to a course.

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a Purchase, which staff then approve or decline.
_Avoid_: Cancel, Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund eligibility**:
Whether a learner may request a Refund for a Purchase: within the refund window, below the watch-time and completion limits, and not a claimed team seat. Learners who are eligible see "Request a refund".
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refund requested**:
The state of a Refund after the learner submits it and before staff decide. Shown to learners as "Pending Refund".
_Avoid_: Pending Chargeback, Reversal requested

**Refund approved**:
A Refund that staff accepted. The learner gets their money back.

**Refund declined**:
A Refund that staff rejected. Nothing is refunded.
